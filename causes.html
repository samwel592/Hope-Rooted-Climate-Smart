Our Causes

Climate-smart agriculture, disability inclusion, environmental conservation, health & nutrition, and poverty relief — the five pillars behind every HOCS program.

Featured Cause

Climate-smart agriculture is at the heart of our work. We train farmers on organic and circular resource use — composting, frass fertilizers, and sustainable water systems — while supporting production, value addition, and market access for African indigenous and exotic vegetables.

This program directly supports smallholder farmers across Siaya, Kisii, Homa-Bay, Kwale and Nakuru counties, helping households build food security and resilient incomes in the face of a changing climate.

Your donation funds training sessions, farm inputs, and demonstration plots that help entire communities adopt climate-resilient practices.
