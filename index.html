Why Choose Us

HOCS is a registered Public Benefit Organization built on inclusivity, transparency and community-driven action — delivering programs that are ethical, participatory and rooted in real evidence from the field.

1

Registered & Compliant

HOCS is registered with the NGOs Co-ordination Board (NGO-EEUWKRY) and operates in full compliance with the Public Benefit Organizations Act, 2013.

2

Community-Led Programs

Our farmers, women and youth groups, and persons with disabilities help shape every program from the ground up, ensuring interventions fit real local needs.

3

Multi-County Reach

Active programs span Siaya, Kisii, Homa-Bay, Kwale and Nakuru counties, with a registered office in Nakuru and field operations rooted in Siaya.

4

Transparent & Accountable

Annual audits, a governed Board of 5–9 members, and clear financial reporting ensure every donation is tracked and put to work responsibly.
